TeamCal

One shared calendar for your team, with color-coded sub-calendars and shareable links — no accounts needed.

You get an administrator link. Bookmark it — it is the only key to manage the calendar.

This copy runs without a server: calendars are stored in this browser only, and links work only on this device. Use Settings → General to back up your data.

Recently opened on this device

    Sub-calendars

    Organize by team, project, room or person. Toggle them on and off, each in its own color.

    Six views

    Day, week, month, year, list and a timeline that lays sub-calendars side by side.

    Access links

    Give each group its own link: admin, modify, add-only or read-only, optionally limited to some sub-calendars.

    Repeating events

    Daily, weekly, monthly and yearly rules; edit one occurrence, the rest of a series, or all.

    Live and connected

    Changes appear instantly in every open tab. Subscribe via iCal in Google, Outlook or Apple Calendar.

    Comments and history

    Discuss events in comment threads; administrators see an activity log of every change.
